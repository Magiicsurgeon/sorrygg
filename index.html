Meri jaan ❤️

Mujhe nahi pata tum abhi mere baare mein kya soch rahi ho, tumhare dil mein mere liye kya hai, aur tum mujhse baat karna bhi chahti ho ya nahi.

Lekin ek baat main dil se kehna chahta hoon...

I'm really sorry, jaan. Sach mein dil se sorry.

Maine tumhare past ke baare mein jo baat ki, jis tarah tumhari purani zindagi aur tumhari galtiyon ka zikr kiya, usse tumhe bohot dukh hua.

Tumhe laga ke main tumhari izzat nahi karta, main bhi tumhe judge kar raha hoon. Jaan, mujhe afsos hai ke meri baat tumhare dil ko lagi.

Mujhe tumhari feelings ko samajhna chahiye tha, na ke sirf apni baat samjhane ki koshish karni chahiye thi.

Tumhari life mein pehle kya hua, kisne tumhe hurt kiya, kisne tumhara trust toda... main nahi chahta ke tumhe woh sab dobara yaad aaye meri wajah se.

Tumne jo kuch saha, uske liye main tumhe kabhi judge nahi karna chahta. Tumhara past tumhari poori pehchaan nahi hai.

Tumhe mere saamne apni worth prove karne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai.

Maine tumse kaha tha ke hum apne past ko peeche chhod kar ek achhi zindagi jee sakte hain.

Main kehna chahta tha ke main tumhare saath future banana chahta hoon, tumhe samajhna chahta hoon aur hum dono milkar apni zindagi behtar bana sakte hain.

Lekin maine woh baat sahi tareeqe se nahi kahi. Mere lafz tumhare dil ko chubh gaye.

Mujhe aisa nahi kehna chahiye tha ke tumhe apni galti accept karni hogi tabhi hum aage badh sakte hain.

Jaan, main tumhe kisi aur se compare nahi karna chahta. Na tumhare past ke logon se, na kisi aur se.

Main yeh nahi chahta ke tum mere saath sirf isliye raho kyunki main tumhe bol raha hoon ke main achha insaan hoon.

Main chahta hoon ke agar tum mere saath raho, toh apni marzi se raho, apne dil se raho, aur tumhe mere saath izzat aur sukoon mehsoos ho.

Tum koi ehsaan nahi ho, jaan. Tum ek insaan ho, apni feelings aur apni izzat ke saath.

Maine tumhara haath pakda kyunki main tumse mohabbat karta hoon. Main tumhare saath khada rehna chahta hoon.

Lekin ab mujhe yeh bhi samajhna hai ke tumhara haath pakadne ka matlab tumhari baat sunna, tumhari feelings ki izzat karna aur tumhe apni baaton se takleef na dene ki koshish karna bhi hai.

Main tumhare past ko badal nahi sakta, lekin main apne aaj ke rawaiye ko behtar karne ki koshish kar sakta hoon.

Main tumse bohot pyaar karta hoon. Yeh baat main jhutla nahi sakta.

Main tumhare saath ek achha future chahta tha, aur dil mein abhi bhi woh khwahish hai. Lekin main yeh bhi samajh raha hoon ke sirf mera pyaar kaafi nahi hai.

Tumhara trust, tumhara sukoon aur tumhari marzi bhi utni hi important hai.

Mohabbat mein apni baat rakhna zaroori hai, lekin saamne wale ke dil ka khayal rakhna bhi zaroori hai.

Agar tum mujhse naraz ho, toh main tumhari baat sunna chahta hoon.

Agar tumhe mujhse kuch kehna hai, toh main bina beech mein apni safai diye sunne ki koshish karunga. Aur agar tumhe waqt chahiye, toh main tumhe force nahi karunga.

Main yeh nahi bolunga ke tum mujhe abhi maaf karo, ya tum apna faisla badal do.

Main bas apni galti ke liye maafi maang raha hoon.

Sorry, meri jaan. Maine tumhe dukh diya, aur mujhe iska afsos hai.

Main chahta hoon ke tum hamesha khush raho, tumhe apni zindagi mein izzat mile, sukoon mile aur tum apne aap ko kisi ke saamne chhota mehsoos na karo.

Main tumhari khushi chahta hoon, aur yeh baat sirf tab nahi jab tum mere saath ho.

Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe. Apna khayal rakhna. ❤️

With love, always. ♡
— Syed with a softer heart
